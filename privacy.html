Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Hey! Chillmail is a simple, calm email app that works with your own Google account. This page explains what it touches and what it does with that info. Short version: everything stays in your browser, and we don't run any servers that collect your stuff.

What Chillmail can access

When you sign in with Google, you give Chillmail permission to:

  • Send emails for you (gmail.send).
  • Read basic email details (gmail.readonly) so your Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam and Bin folders can show up. That means sender, recipient, subject, date and a short snippet.

Chillmail never sees your Google password. Sign-in happens entirely on Google's side.

Where your data lives

Chillmail doesn't have its own servers and doesn't store your data anywhere else. Everything is saved in your browser's local storage, including:

  • Your Google sign-in token (it expires after about an hour) and your Client ID.
  • A cached copy of the folder info listed above.
  • Your contacts, drafts, and scheduled messages.
  • Your name, profile picture and theme colours.

Clear the site data in your browser and it's all gone.

What we do with it

Your email is only used to do what you ask: send messages, show your folders, and search what's cached on your device. We don't sell it, share it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI.

Who gets to see it

The only thing Chillmail talks to is Google's Gmail service, and only to do what you've asked. No third parties, no analytics, no ad trackers.

Google's rules

Chillmail follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Revoke access any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Delete local data by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Keeping things safe

Since your data lives in your browser, it's only as safe as your device. Don't use Chillmail on a shared or public computer.

Kids

Chillmail isn't made for kids under 13, and we don't knowingly collect info from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
