What Chillmail is

Chillmail is a free, browser-based email app. It connects to your own Gmail and Outlook accounts through Google's and Microsoft's services, and lets you read, send and reply to mail, manage contacts, write drafts, schedule messages, set simple email rules, and personalise how it looks. It also has a tutorial, sounds, notifications and a few extras.

Sending, replying and scheduling

You're responsible for what you send. Chillmail gives you a short undo window before a message goes out, but once Gmail or Outlook accepts it, Chillmail can't take it back.

Scheduled messages only go out while Chillmail is open in a browser tab. If the tab is closed, the device is off or offline, or you're signed out, they won't send until you're back. Scheduled messages with attachments keep those files in your browser.

We can't guarantee delivery. Gmail, Outlook or the recipient's provider might delay, filter or reject a message.

Email rules and bulk actions

Email rules, and the mark read, unread, Spam and Delete buttons, change your mail on your Gmail or Outlook account. Rules run automatically on new mail when Chillmail syncs. Please set them carefully: a rule that matches too much can move mail to Spam or Bin. Mail moved to Bin can usually be recovered from there, but it may be deleted later by your provider.

Contacts

If you import your Google contacts, Chillmail reads them to show in search and in your contacts list. Imported contacts stay in your browser, and you can remove them by clearing this site's data.

Attachments and limits

Attachments are stored only in your browser. Clearing site data, or using another device or browser, removes them. Gmail accepts emails up to about 25 MB in total, and Outlook accepts attachments up to about 3 MB each for sending from Chillmail. Limits set by Google, Microsoft or the recipient's provider always apply.
