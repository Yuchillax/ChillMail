Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Hey! Chillmail is a calm email app that runs in your web browser and works with your own Gmail and Outlook accounts. This page explains what it can access, where your information is kept, and which outside services it talks to.

The short version

  • Chillmail doesn't have its own servers and doesn't collect your information.
  • Your email, contacts, settings and drafts stay in your browser.
  • Chillmail talks directly to Google and Microsoft to read and send your mail, and to a few other services listed below.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, use analytics, or use your email to train AI.

What Chillmail can access

You choose what to connect. Chillmail only asks for the permissions it needs:

Gmail accounts (through Google)

  • Send email (gmail.send) for messages and replies you write.
  • Read and change your mail (gmail.modify): reading messages and folders, marking mail read or unread, and moving mail to Spam or Bin when you ask (including through email rules).
  • Read your contacts (contacts.readonly), only when you press Import Google contacts.

Outlook and Hotmail accounts (through Microsoft)

  • Read and change your mail (Mail.ReadWrite, Mail.Read): reading messages and folders, marking mail read or unread, and moving mail.
  • Send email (Mail.Send).
  • Your basic profile (User.Read), so Chillmail can show which account you're using.

Chillmail never sees your Google or Microsoft password. Signing in happens on the provider's own page.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored in your browser, not on our servers:

Anything saved in your browser stays there until you delete it. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it from that device, and it can't be recovered by us.

How your information is used

Your email and contacts are used only to do what you ask: show your folders, send and reply, schedule messages, mark or move mail, run the rules you set, and search. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Outside services the app uses

Chillmail is built to avoid unnecessary outside services, but a few are needed:

  • Google: sign-in, Gmail and Google Contacts. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft: sign-in, Outlook and Hotmail mail. Governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts: the Inter font is loaded from Google when the page opens.
  • DiceBear: when there's no profile photo, a generated avatar is requested from dicebear.com. The request includes the person's name or email address, which is used to create the avatar.
  • Microsoft sign-in library and Google sign-in script: loaded from Microsoft and Google so that sign-in works.
  • Google Translate: used only when you choose a language other than English. Your language choice is kept in a small cookie on this site, so the page stays in that language.

Notifications and sounds are created by your browser or device, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Chillmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Remove an account in Chillmail's Settings to forget it on this device.
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or from your Microsoft account's app permissions.
  • Delete local data by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Turn off notifications, sounds and the penguin in Personalise.

Keeping things safe

Because your information is stored in your browser, it's only as safe as your device and browser. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to read it. Don't use Chillmail on a shared or public computer, and sign out of your accounts if you're finished.

Children

Chillmail isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
